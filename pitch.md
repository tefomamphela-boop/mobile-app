# ReCircle — 2-minute investor pitch

**Opening / problem (0:00–0:25)**  
Imagine wanting to recycle a plastic bottle but not knowing where to take it, what the local collection point accepts, or whether your effort makes a difference. Many people want cleaner neighbourhoods, yet recycling information can be scattered and participation can be hard to sustain.

**Solution (0:25–0:50)**  
ReCircle is a simple, mobile-friendly app that makes recycling easier and more rewarding. It brings community drop-off points and collection events into one place, lets people log recyclable items, and shows their progress through points and estimated material diverted.

**Target users and features (0:50–1:15)**  
Our first users are students, households, schools, and community groups. Key features include a local recycling directory, a quick item logger, a points dashboard, and community collection information. In the prototype, users can add plastic bottles, aluminium cans, paper bundles, and glass bottles to see their points update instantly.

**Value and business model (1:15–1:40)**  
ReCircle creates value by reducing the effort needed to recycle and helping people build a repeatable habit. Community partners can use it to promote collection drives and understand participation. The app could remain free for residents, while organisations may pay for campaign dashboards, and local businesses could sponsor clearly labelled rewards.

**Competitive difference and close (1:40–2:00)**  
Unlike a static list of bins, ReCircle combines discovery, action tracking, motivation, and community participation in one experience. We would start with a small pilot, verify collection partners, test which rewards work, and measure repeat participation. ReCircle turns everyday recycling into visible community progress. Thank you.

## Presenter note
Speak naturally and aim for about 230–260 words over two minutes. The prototype uses fictional example locations and illustrative impact estimates; explain that these would be verified before a real launch.
